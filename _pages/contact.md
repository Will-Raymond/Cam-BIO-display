We are an interdisciplinary team of scientists with backgrounds that range from protein engineering, virology, fluorescence microscopy, machine learning, and RNA biology, aiming to bring efficient and reproducible automated laboratory experiments to Colorado State University, and to the broader scientific world!

## Contact us!

If you would like to contact us about using automation at Cam-BIO for your experiment, please email us. (A shared cambio@colostate.edu address is coming soon.)
